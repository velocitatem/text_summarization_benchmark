Understanding the Different Summarization Methods

Extractive Summarization (TextRank):

This method selects existing sentences from the text based on their importance. It uses a graph-based ranking algorithm (similar to Google's PageRank) to identify the most central sentences in the text. The advantage is that it preserves the original wording, but it may miss connections between ideas.

Seq2Seq Summarization (T5):

This is a generative approach that uses the T5 (Text-To-Text Transfer Transformer) model to create new text that summarizes the content. T5 treats every NLP task as a text-to-text problem, allowing it to generate summaries that may rephrase or combine ideas from the original text.

Fine-tuned Summarization (BART):

BART (Bidirectional and Auto-Regressive Transformers) is a state-of-the-art model pre-trained specifically for text generation tasks. The version used here has been fine-tuned on CNN/DailyMail news articles, making it particularly good at summarizing news and informational content.
